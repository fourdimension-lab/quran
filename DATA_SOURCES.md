# Data Sources, Licensing & Attributions — Al-Quran Platform

The Al-Quran platform relies exclusively on verified, authenticated, and properly attributed datasets to preserve the sanctity of the Holy Quran and uphold academic honesty.

---

## 1. Canonical Quran Arabic Text

- **Source**: Tanzil.net Verified Quran Text Project.
- **Orthography**: Standard Hafs 'an 'Asim (Uthmani Script).
- **Integrity**: 114 Surahs, 6,236 Ayahs, validated by MD5/SHA256 text checksums.
- **Licensing**: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License.
- **Integrity Rule**: No generative AI model or human editor is permitted to alter canonical Arabic glyphs.

---

## 2. Quran Translations

### English: Saheeh International
- **Translators**: Emily Assami, Mary Kennedy, Amatullah Bantley.
- **Scholarly Authority**: Widely accepted contemporary Sunni translation praised for fidelity to classical Arabic syntax.

### Bangla: Taisirul Quran & Maulana Muhiuddin Khan
- **Translators**: Maulana Muhiuddin Khan (Khulasa-e-Tafsir) and Taisirul Quran (Tawheed Publications).
- **Authority**: The standard authoritative Bengali translation recognized across Bangladesh and the Bengali-speaking Ummah.

---

## 3. Word-by-Word Morphology & Roots

- **Source**: Quranic Arabic Corpus (University of Leeds, Language Research Group).
- **Attributes**: Parts of speech, root letters, lemma, case endings (I'rab), and morphological affixation.
- **Licensing**: Open academic research license.

---

## 4. Tafsir (Exegesis) Works

- **Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Abridged)**: Classic exegesis by Al-Hafidh Imaduddin Ismail ibn Kathir (d. 774 AH).
- **Tafsir Ahsanul Bayaan**: Authored by Hafiz Salahuddin Yusuf, revised and annotated in Bengali by Dr. Abu Bakr Muhammad Zakaria.
- **Tafsir al-Jalalayn**: Authored by Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli and completed by Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti.
